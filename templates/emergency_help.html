🩹 Quick First-Aid Guide

Basic items and information for common situations.

🩹 Cuts & Wounds

  • Band-Aids / Bandages
  • Antiseptic solution
  • Sterile cotton & gauze

🔥 Minor Burns

  • Cool the burn with clean running water.
  • Use a sterile non-stick dressing.
  • Seek medical help for serious burns.

🦴 Sprains & Pains

  • Crape bandage
  • Instant ice pack
  • Rest the affected area

🧰 Essential First-Aid Tools

  • Medical scissors
  • Digital thermometer
  • Safety pins & gloves
