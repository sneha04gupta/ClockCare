Healthy Eating

Eat a balanced diet with fruits, vegetables, whole grains and nutritious foods.

  • Eat fresh fruits and vegetables
  • Choose nutritious meals
  • Limit junk and processed food
  • Drink enough water
🏃

Physical Activity

Regular physical activity helps keep your body strong and active.

  • Exercise regularly
  • Take short walks
  • Stretch your body
  • Avoid sitting for long periods
😴

Good Sleep

Proper sleep helps your body recover and keeps your mind fresh.

  • Maintain a regular sleep schedule
  • Avoid screens before sleeping
  • Keep your room comfortable
  • Get enough sleep every night
🧘

Mental Wellness

Taking care of your mental health is just as important as taking care of your body.

  • Take breaks when needed
  • Practice relaxation techniques
  • Spend time with loved ones
  • Talk to someone when you need help

💡 Daily Health Tip

Drink enough water, stay active, eat nutritious food and give your body enough time to rest.
